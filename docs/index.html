      About and methodology

      Where Are the Carriers? is a public-source assessment of U.S. aircraft carrier locations. It is not live tracking, and positions are approximate.

      • Source pages are parsed from public text where carrier names, dates, and locations are available.
      • Public tracker-map images from supported sources are parsed to estimate approximate positions.
      • Positions can lag real ship movements and should be treated as broad location assessments.
      • Last reported shows the newest source date that supports the displayed assessment.
